**The Velvet Vault** — a top-down museum heist game set in the Aurelian Museum after closing. You are Foxglove, a gentleman thief: cross the galleries, lift the Sun Mask, the Jade Serpent and the Imperial Egg, take the Aurelian Star from its plinth, and escape up the skylight rope in the entrance hall. Three guards patrol with flashlight cones and two cameras sweep the halls; display cases, pillars and walls block the light, so timing and cover are everything. Walking makes noise ripples that draw a guard to investigate — hold Shift to tiptoe in silence. Standing in a cone or crossing a live laser gate raises the alarm; at 50% the guards go alert, at 100% the heist fails and the loot resets. Loot value, treasures, times spotted and a rank — Ghost, Master Thief, Cat Burglar or Smash and Grab — are scored on the win screen. All art is flat canvas drawing and all sound is synthesized WebAudio — no assets, no network, it plays offline in a desktop browser. The screenshots in `screenshots/` are real renders of the game, staged by its deterministic `?shot=title|play|alert|win` modes.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
